How to Add Your Video Editing Projects & Showreels

STEP 1

Drop Your Video Files in the videos/ Folder

Export your video edit or showreel as a web-optimized .mp4 or .webm (H.264/AAC, 1080p or 4K, 10–60s preview), then place it in the portfolio's videos/ folder:

videos/edit-reels.mp4       ← (Viral Reels & TikToks)
videos/edit-commercial.mp4  ← (Brand Commercials & Promos)
videos/edit-drone.mp4       ← (FPV & Aerial Cinematography)
videos/edit-vfx.mp4         ← (3D Motion Graphics & VFX)
